# Glossary

Terms and abbreviations used in the ITYC tools. Each definition links to an explanation or tutorial in the documentation.

<!-- anchor:glossary-auto-sails -->
## Automatic sails

Mode in which the game selects and changes sails according to conditions, equipment and available sails. Also called “Auto Sails” or “auto sail”, it uses a speed margin to avoid repeated sail changes.

[Understand automatic sails](#aide?article=tuto_autoSail&section=tp_p1).

<!-- anchor:glossary-best-vmg -->
## Best VMG (BVMG)

Best achievable VMG upwind or downwind for a given wind speed and configuration. BVMG indicators include the optimal wind angle in degrees (°), which does not necessarily produce the highest boat speed.

[Read the best VMG charts](#aide?article=tuto_polaire&section=8-bestvmg-wind-angle-f-tws-twa-for-best-vmg).

<!-- anchor:glossary-distance-lost -->
## Distance lost

Distance deficit caused by slowing down during a manoeuvre, compared with a boat that keeps its speed without manoeuvring. The calculator expresses it in nautical miles (nm), using boat speed and the penalty.

[Use the penalty calculator](#aide?article=tuto_pena&section=tp_p4_3).

<!-- anchor:glossary-double-penalty -->
## Double penalty

Penalty associated with a sail change performed at the same time as a tack or gybe. The dedicated tool evaluates penalty time and distance lost for this combination of manoeuvres.

[Calculate a double penalty](#aide?article=tuto_pena&section=tp_p6).

<!-- anchor:glossary-dtf -->
## DTF (Distance To Finish)

Distance to the finish, displayed in nautical miles (nm) in the Dashboard. This indicator describes the remaining distance for the boat concerned.

[Read the race status](#aide?article=aide_dash&section=tabDashTab3).

<!-- anchor:glossary-dtu -->
## DTU (Distance To You)

Distance between your boat and a boat in the fleet list, expressed in nautical miles (nm). “Distance To yoU” is the label used in the Dashboard.

[Read the fleet indicators](#aide?article=aide_dash&section=tabDashTab5).

<!-- anchor:glossary-energy-recovery -->
## Energy recovery

The skipper’s energy, or stamina, increasing over time. The tool shows the time needed to recover one energy point and to reach a target level, depending on wind conditions.

[Estimate recovery time](#aide?article=tuto_energy&section=tp_p5_1).

<!-- anchor:glossary-foils -->
## Foils

Boat equipment included in speed calculations. Its effect depends on wind conditions; the polar analyser can display the foil usage range and activation percentage.

[Display foils in the analyser](#aide?article=tuto_polaire&section=5-polar-analyser).

<!-- anchor:glossary-gybe -->
## Gybe (jibe)

A change of tack with the stern passing through the wind direction, also called “jibe”. ITYC describes its effects through penalty time, distance lost and energy loss.

[Compare manoeuvre penalties](#aide?article=tuto_pena&section=tp_p4).

<!-- anchor:glossary-hdg -->
## HDG (Heading)

The boat’s course, expressed in degrees (°). HDG describes the direction the boat is heading, while TWA describes its angle relative to the wind.

[Read the navigation measurements](#aide?article=aide_dash&section=ingratedGameTab3).

<!-- anchor:glossary-knot -->
## Knot (kt, kts)

Unit of speed used for both boat and wind speed. Interfaces use abbreviations including “kt” and “kts”; French documentation also uses “nd” and “nds” for “nœud” or “noeud”.

[Read speeds and navigation measurements](#aide?article=aide_dash&section=ingratedGameTab3).

<!-- anchor:glossary-nautical-mile -->
## Nautical mile (nm)

Unit of distance used in ITYC for distance travelled, distance lost and remaining distance. It is abbreviated “nm” in the Dashboard and called “mille nautique” or “mile nautique” in French.

[Read Dashboard distances](#aide?article=aide_dash&section=tabDashTab3).

<!-- anchor:glossary-optimal-sail -->
## Optimal sail

Fastest sail among the selected sails for a given TWS, TWA and configuration. The Sail usage map represents it with a colour; automatic mode may retain another sail because of its change margin.

[Read the sail usage map](#aide?article=tuto_polaire&section=12-sail-usage-map).

<!-- anchor:glossary-overlap -->
## Overlap zone

TWS and TWA conditions in which the automatic change margin can retain the current sail despite another sail being theoretically faster. The overlap coefficient, also described as an overspeed margin, is part of this comparison.

[Understand overlap between sails](#aide?article=tuto_autoSail&section=7-overlap-zone-between-sails).

<!-- anchor:glossary-polar -->
## Polar

Set of data describing boat speed according to wind speed (TWS), wind angle (TWA), sail and selected options. The analyser’s polar curves and maps represent these performance data.

[Understand polars](#aide?article=tuto_polaire&section=tp_p1).

<!-- anchor:glossary-polish -->
## Polish

Hull preparation option, also called “hull polish”. Enabling it changes the calculated performance; select the same option in ITYC as on the boat being compared.

[Select boat options](#aide?article=tuto_polaire&section=tp_p3).

<!-- anchor:glossary-sail-change -->
## Sail change

Manoeuvre that replaces the active sail with another sail, manually or through automatic sails. It causes a penalty and energy loss, which should be included when comparing the expected speed gain.

[Calculate the effects of a sail change](#aide?article=tuto_pena&section=tp_p4).

<!-- anchor:glossary-break-even -->
## Sail-change break-even point

Time or distance needed for a new sail’s speed gain to compensate for the loss incurred while changing it. This break-even point compares distance travelled with and without a sail change.

[Evaluate whether a manoeuvre pays off](#aide?article=tuto_pena&section=tp_p5).

<!-- anchor:glossary-simple-penalty -->
## Simple penalty

Temporary slowdown associated with one manoeuvre: a tack, gybe or sail change. ITYC calculates its duration in minutes and seconds, then the distance lost according to boat speed.

[Calculate a simple penalty](#aide?article=tuto_pena&section=tp_p4).

<!-- anchor:glossary-stamina -->
## Stamina (skipper energy)

The skipper’s energy level, expressed as a percentage (%). Manoeuvres consume energy, and this level affects their duration through a penalty multiplier.

[Understand the effect of stamina](#aide?article=aide_pena&section=tp_p3).

<!-- anchor:glossary-tack -->
## Tack

A change of tack with the bow passing through the wind direction, also called “tacking”. ITYC describes its effects through penalty time, distance lost and energy loss.

[Compare manoeuvre penalties](#aide?article=tuto_pena&section=tp_p4).

<!-- anchor:glossary-twa -->
## TWA (True Wind Angle)

Angle between the boat’s heading and the true wind direction, expressed in degrees (°), also called wind angle. Together with TWS, it sets the conditions used to read polars and select a sail.

[Read TWA in the navigation measurements](#aide?article=aide_dash&section=ingratedGameTab3).

<!-- anchor:glossary-twd -->
## TWD (True Wind Direction)

Direction of the true wind at the boat’s position, expressed in degrees (°). It provides the reference for locating the wind relative to the boat’s heading.

[Read wind direction](#aide?article=aide_dash&section=ingratedGameTab3).

<!-- anchor:glossary-tws -->
## TWS (True Wind Speed)

Speed of the true wind, expressed in knots (kt or kts). It is one of the parameters that determine boat speed, penalties and energy recovery times.

[Read wind speed](#aide?article=aide_dash&section=ingratedGameTab3).

<!-- anchor:glossary-vmg -->
## VMG (Velocity Made Good)

Useful speed component along the wind axis, expressed in knots (kts). It measures upwind or downwind progress and depends on boat speed as well as wind angle.

[Understand VMG and optimal angles](#aide?article=tuto_polaire&section=8-bestvmg-wind-angle-f-tws-twa-for-best-vmg).
